Uforiya
Every track. Every version. Every collaborator. Nothing lost.

When people make music together, files get sent back and forth and pile up in folders with names like final_v3_REALFINAL. Nobody is sure which one is the latest. Uforiya gives every song one home: every version, everyone working on it, and all of their feedback in one place. It's made for artists, producers, songwriters and studios working together from anywhere.

What it does

Upload a song

Drop in any common audio file and Uforiya works out its speed, musical key and length for you.

Every version saved

First draft, updated draft, final version. They all stay together, and the owner picks which one is the real one.

Invite your team

Add people by username or email and choose what each person can do. Everyone gets credit for their part.

Feedback at the exact moment

Leave a comment at the exact second of the song you mean, so notes are never confusing.

Private sessions Pro

A private space for a studio day. Share one link, everyone uploads to the same place, and lock it when you're done.

Free audio tools

Cut, fade, speed up, change pitch or change the file type right in your browser. Some tools don't even need an account.

How it's built

I built Uforiya on my own and started with the foundation: how data is stored, who can see what, and how files stay safe. Only then did I design the screens.

  • Next.js and React (TypeScript) for the app
  • Supabase and PostgreSQL for accounts and data
  • Cloudflare R2 for storing audio files
  • A Python service that reads each song's speed and key
  • Stripe for Pro payments

Sample Organizer Desktop

A free desktop app for producers. Producers buy lots of sound packs, and the same sounds often show up in many of them under different names. This app finds the exact copies and clears them out. On one real library it found 443MB of copies in about one second.

  • Finds true copies, even when the names are different
  • Runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, no account needed
  • Never deletes your last copy, and removed files can be restored
  • Built with Electron and open source
